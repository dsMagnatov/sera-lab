  • Ferritin

    Measures how long blood takes to clot.

    Measures the level of vitamin B9 in blood.

Membership questions

Before your visit.

What’s the difference between Essential and Plus?

Essential includes one checkup a year with a focused set of tests. Plus includes two checkups and adds thyroid, blood sugar, iron and vitamin markers.

Which tests are included in each package?

Each package lists its included tests above. The blood test catalog explains what each panel measures.

How do I prepare for my blood draw?

Preparation depends on the tests included in your visit. Check your appointment instructions or ask the lab what applies to your panel.

When will I get my results?

Turnaround varies by test. Ask the lab for the expected timing for your panel when you arrange your visit.

Who can help me understand my results?

Bring your results to a healthcare professional who can interpret them alongside your medical history.
